## Company summary

Grade is a B2B company offering a payout API that simplifies creator payments, compliance, and operational tasks like KYC and tax reporting, particularly for marketplaces and agencies dealing with international transactions.

## Pain points

- Complexity in managing international payment compliance for a large number of creators.
- Need for rapid resolution of compliance or payment-related issues to avoid service interruptions.
- Desire to minimize engineering overhead associated with maintaining in-house payment solutions.

## Value props

- Faster incident root-cause analysis for small engineering teams.
- Surfaces hypotheses from logs, deploys, and runbooks without a full SRE org.
- Fits teams that already own on-call but lack dedicated reliability headcount.
