## Summary

Envariant is creating an interpretability SDK designed for foundation models, addressing the needs of B2B customers to manage and control model behavior effectively. The SDK provides primitives for model inspection and steering to address issues like hallucination and invariant violations. While Envariant demonstrates a strong focus on usability through API documentation, the absence of established reliability protocols or on-call processes could be a concern for teams seeking robust production-critical solutions.
