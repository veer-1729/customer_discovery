## Summary

Archal has developed a platform focused on the pre-deployment evaluation of autonomous AI agents using stateful clones of existing systems like GitHub and Slack, allowing for detailed testing and tracing of agent behavior before production. This solution is particularly suited for software development and engineering teams aiming to ensure the reliability and functionality of AI agents before deployment. Archal's methodologies include using markdown scenarios for simulating agent operations and evaluating them against digital twins with deterministic and probabilistic scoring metrics, thereby facilitating confident and reliable agent deployment.
