## Summary

Civys provides AI-driven pre-compliance intelligence by converting legal and policy texts into detailed, audit-ready briefs. It categorizes obligations, traces claims to source texts, and highlights gaps or uncertainties, which can significantly reduce the risks of misinterpretation for organizations dealing with complex regulatory environments. The platform's API facilitates integration, allowing seamless access to legislative data and enhancing application capabilities. Civys is well-suited for integration with Emergent Delta's incident RCA solution due to its production-critical features evidenced through its robust API and the operational language around legislative text interpretation and compliance tracking.
